# Code of Conduct

VetTV MCE is built and maintained by a U.S. military veteran and content creator, in public, with retro-gaming and creator communities watching. The bar for behavior in this repo and any associated space is simple.

## The bar

- **Be useful.** Bring information, not heat.
- **Be honest.** Don't pad, don't posture, don't manufacture drama.
- **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- **Stay on-topic.** This repo is about shipping a console OS.

## Not tolerated

- Harassment, slurs, threats, or targeted personal attacks
- Politicized brigading unrelated to the project
- Discrimination on any of the usual axes
- Doxxing or sharing private information without consent
- Sexualized content or advances
- Persistent off-topic disruption after a warning

## Enforcement

The maintainer (Vet) is the sole moderator until the project grows past single-maintainer scope. Enforcement actions, in order of severity:

1. Comment removed, public note explaining why
2. Temporary block from the repo
3. Permanent block, with a referenced reason

There is no appeal queue. If you think a call was wrong, open a respectful issue or DM `@Vet_TV` on X.

## Scope

This applies to issues, PRs, discussions, commit messages, and any official VetTV MCE space (Discord channel, X replies tagged to project posts, etc.).

## Reporting

Contact `@Vet_TV` on X via DM, or open a private security advisory if the conduct involves information disclosure. Do not file conduct reports as public issues.
